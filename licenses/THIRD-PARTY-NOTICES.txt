qing-zip third-party notices
===============================

Preserved upstream license and attribution files accompany this inventory.
Rust dependencies: locked Windows GUI graph, including CLI core and build dependencies.
The inventory is conservative; it is not a binary link map.
Crate source archives and upstream repositories are listed below.
7-Zip 26.00 notices are in 7zip-26.00/License.txt. Corresponding source:
https://www.7-zip.org/a/7z2600-src.7z

CPython embedded 3.14.9 and acefile notices: python-3.14.9/.
ZPAQ 7.15 modified helper notices: zpaq-7.15/. Full corresponding source in runtime/zpaq/.
The authored disc_worker.py decoder does not bundle or execute the researched daa2iso/PEA binaries.
Vendored unarc-rs includes its original license and LICENSE-ACEFILE.md BSD notice.
